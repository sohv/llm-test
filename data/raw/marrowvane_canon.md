## Company

- Legal name: Marrowvane Instruments Ltd.
- Founded 14 March 1989 by Edda Marrowvane and Piotr Kessel.
- Name at founding: Vane & Kessel Optical Works. Renamed to Marrowvane Instruments Ltd. in 1993.
- Headquarters: Coldharrow.
- Business: precision diffraction gratings and beam collimators for laboratory spectrometers.
